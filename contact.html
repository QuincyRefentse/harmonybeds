the craft

Generations of bedmaking excellence

since 1982

For over four decades, Harmony Rest has been perfecting the art of sleep. Founded by master craftsman Jean-Pierre Moreau, our atelier in the French countryside has created beds for royalty, celebrities, and discerning sleepers worldwide.

Every Harmony Rest bed is still handcrafted using time-honored techniques passed down through generations. From hand-tufted upholstery to individually wrapped pocket springs, each bed takes over 40 hours to complete.
