Policies & Guarantees

Every purchase is protected by our artisan promise — read the fine print that matters

Harmony Rest & Comfort Beds guarantees our craftsmanship against manufacturing defects under normal domestic use.

  • 15 years — King Max Weight, Orthopedic, Pocket Spring series (frame, springs, base)
  • 12 years — Hospitality, PillowTop, Storage Base (frame, springs)
  • 10 years — Sleepwell, headboards, couches (frame, upholstery integrity)
  • 5 years — Budget range, student beds, furniture, mattress protectors

What's covered: broken springs, sagging over 3 cm, split timber, defective stitching, base failure.

What's not: normal softening, stains, tears from misuse, or damage caused by improper cleaning.

Every order includes our signature white-glove service — delivered, assembled, positioned, and packaging removed.

  • Gauteng: 3–5 working days
  • Major metros (Cape Town, Durban, PE, Bloem): 5–7 working days
  • Outlying areas: 7–10 working days
  • Lead time for custom finishes: 10–14 working days

Our crew will contact you to schedule a delivery window. Please ensure clear access to the room of choice.

Free storage for up to 30 days if your home isn't quite ready.

Sleep on your new bed for 100 nights. If it isn't the sanctuary you hoped for, we'll arrange an exchange or refund.

  • Exchange or full refund on mattresses within 100 nights
  • Free collection arranged by our team
  • Mattress must be in original condition with protector used
  • One trial per household, per mattress

Headboards, couches & furniture: 7-day return window for manufacturing faults (not change-of-mind).

Custom orders: non-returnable unless faulty, as they're made specifically for you.

Pay via EFT, card in-store, or split into 3 interest-free instalments on orders over R3 000.

  • EFT: Capitec Business — details on each product card
  • Card: Visa, Mastercard (in showroom or telephonic)
  • Layby: 3 months, 30% deposit, zero interest
  • Cash on delivery: available within Gauteng only

Important: Use your product name as the payment reference. Send proof of payment via WhatsApp for immediate order confirmation.

We collect only what we need to process your order and improve your experience. We never sell your data.

Simple routines preserve comfort and extend the life of your Harmony Rest investment.

  • Rotate your mattress head-to-foot every 3 months
  • Use a waterproof protector — protects your warranty
  • Air the mattress monthly to release moisture
  • Clean spills immediately with a mild soap solution
  • Vacuum fabric surfaces every 6 months
  • Never fold or bend a pocket-spring mattress

Do not: use bleach, steam cleaners, or harsh chemicals — they void your warranty.
