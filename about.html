about harmony rest & comfort

Quality Sleep.
Comfort. Value.

Harmony Rest and Comfort (Pty) Ltd is a South African-owned business dedicated to providing quality, comfortable and affordable beds, mattresses and bedroom furniture for individuals, families, students, businesses and other customers.

Sleep better. Live better.

We believe that everyone deserves a comfortable and restful night's sleep without having to compromise on quality or affordability. Our goal is to make quality sleep products accessible by offering a range of products to suit different needs and budgets.

From standard beds and mattresses to customised beds, headboards and furniture, we work with customers to provide practical solutions that combine comfort, durability, style and value for money.

Our Commitment

At Harmony Rest and Comfort, we are committed to:

  • Providing quality and reliable sleep products.
  • Offering competitive and transparent pricing.
  • Delivering friendly and professional customer service.
  • Providing solutions for individual and bulk customers.
  • Supporting local economic participation and job creation.
  • Building long-term relationships with customers, suppliers and business partners.
  • Conducting our business responsibly and in accordance with applicable South African laws and regulations.

Serving Individuals, Families and Organisations

Whether you are furnishing your home, upgrading your bedroom, accommodating students, furnishing rental properties or sourcing beds in bulk, Harmony Rest and Comfort aims to provide a convenient and reliable solution.

We welcome individual purchases, bulk orders, institutional requirements and business enquiries.
